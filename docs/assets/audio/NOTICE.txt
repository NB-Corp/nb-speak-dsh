AI-Synthesized Demo Audio Notice
================================

All static `.mp3` voice-note demonstration clips in this directory (`casual-en.mp3`,
`casual-zh.mp3`, `sql-en.mp3`, `sql-zh.mp3`, `kanban-en.mp3`, `kanban-zh.mp3`,
`persona-en.mp3`, `persona-zh.mp3`) are AI-generated synthetic speech created solely
to demonstrate `@nb-corp/nb-speak-dsh` complementary voice-note playback inside
DeepSeek Harness (`DSH`) WebUI:

- Engine: ElevenLabs (`eleven_v4`) across both English and Mandarin Chinese clips,
  loudness-normalized to `-16 LUFS`.
- Voices: Official ElevenLabs premade platform voices (`Jessica`, `Sarah`, `Julia`,
  `ZiYue`, and `ShanShan`) under a paid Creator subscription.

Zero real-person voice cloning or impersonation was used.
